# Surface: o jogo inteiro (index.html)

Mode: Experience. The game itself leads from the first viewport.

Audience: course students in teams of 1–4 around one shared screen on Google Meet. Job: each player takes their own decision, sees it play out on the terrain, and writes their own conclusion.

Constraints: static HTML/JS, Three.js from CDN, GitHub Pages. No mock people or default PINs. Free fonts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The game is the operation's map sheet. The terrain is a topographic sheet in relief (sepia contours, UTM grid, blue water), with the decisions drawn by grease pencil on an acetate overlay. It refuses the neon sci-fi HUD and the side-menu quiz.

OWN-WORLD: Map paper #EFE6CF, sepia contours #8A5A2B, UTM ink #1D1D1B, water #1F5FA8 for lines and labels (fill tinted to #5B8FC9 so the 3D river stays legible under hillshade), vegetation tint #C9D6A3, hostile red grease #C8302A, friendly blue grease #1F4FA8. Panels are acetate sheets with registration crosses and a title block (legenda). Units are APP-6 frames: blue rectangle for friendly, red diamond for hostile. Miniatures sit on the relief like a sand table. Type: Barlow Condensed for map titles, Barlow for body, Caveat Brush for grease marks.

STORY: Players learn the situation from the map itself, take control with their PIN, pick a card, and watch it play out on the relief. A loss takes the whole screen in hostile red. A win is marked in blue. They end on a field report written in their own words.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The full-bleed 3D relief sheet with a slow drift, and the map collar strip along the top edge. On the left, the title is set like a sheet title, with one line under it and the Jogar button circled in grease pencil. Bottom: three drawn steps (pick operator + PIN, pick a card on the map, beat the 45:00 clock and write the report).

FORM: Carta Topográfica com Calco, IMPECCABLE'S PICK (my #1 grounded candidate). seed:9b3b75d2f0a84d92. Raises:
- From the night cityscape: the clock dims the map from daylight to a red night map-light.
- From the film rail: mission state is a mark on the rail (X done, struck, flag), not a hue.
- From the brick instructions: how-to-play is drawn diagrams.
- From the ink basin: every readout uses its honest physical unit.
- From the seedbed: a loss card commits the whole field to hostile red.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
